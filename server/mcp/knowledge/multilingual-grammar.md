# Multilingual and Grammar Guidelines (EN, FR, RU, ES)

Guidelines for high-precision, grammatically flawless communication across English, French, Russian, and Spanish.

## English (EN)
- **Grammar**: Perfect subject-verb agreement, accurate verb tenses, proper prepositions (*in, on, at*).
- **Punctuation**: Serial/Oxford comma when listing, clear hyphenation for compound adjectives, apostrophe placement for possessives (*its* vs *it's*).
- **Structure**: Clear topic sentences, active voice preferred, logically connected paragraphs.

## French (Français - FR)
- **Accents & Orthography**: Never omit accents: aigu (*é*), grave (*è, à, ù*), circonflexe (*ê, â, î, ô, û*), tréma (*ë, ï*), cédille (*ç*).
- **Agreements**: Verify noun-adjective gender and number agreements (*féminin, masculin, singulier, pluriel*). Verify auxiliary verbs (*être* vs *avoir*) and past participle agreement rules.
- **Punctuation**: Follow French typographical standards: insert a space before double punctuation marks (*:*, *;*, *?*, *!*), and use proper quotation marks (*« ... »* or standard quotes).
- **Tone**: Maintain appropriate politeness (*vousvoiement* by default for helpful and respectful interaction).

## Russian (Русский - RU)
- **Case System**: Ensure correct declension for nouns, adjectives, and pronouns across all six Russian cases (Nominative, Genitive, Dative, Accusative, Instrumental, Prepositional).
- **Verbal Aspect**: Strictly differentiate between imperfective (ongoing/repeated action) and perfective (completed/single action) verb aspects.
- **Punctuation & Syntax**: Russian punctuation is strictly syntactic. Always place commas before subordinating clauses and relative pronouns (*что, чтобы, который, если, когда, как, так как*).
- **Tone**: Cultured, articulate, natural, using polite address (*Вы/вы* when addressing the user).

## Spanish (Español - ES)
- **Diacritics & Punctuation**: Always apply question marks and exclamation marks at both ends: opening (*¿*, *¡*) and closing (*?*, *!*). Preserve *tildes* (*á, é, í, ó, ú*) and the letter *ñ*.
- **Grammar Essentials**:
  - Accurate distinction between *ser* (inherent essence, identity, time) and *estar* (state, condition, location).
  - Accurate usage of *por* (cause, motive, duration, means) vs *para* (purpose, destination, deadline).
  - Proper use of the subjunctive mood (*subjuntivo*) when expressing doubt, desire, hypothetical conditions, or emotion.
  - Concordance of gender and number (*los problemas difíciles*, *las soluciones prácticas*).
- **Tone**: Fluent, friendly yet professional, using natural phrasing without robotic calques from other languages.
