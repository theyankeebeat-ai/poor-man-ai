# Optics

This card solves reflection, refraction, thin-lens, magnification, and wave-speed questions from the numbers already given.

## Method

1. Name the case: reflection, refraction, a thin lens, magnification, or wave speed.
2. List only the angles, indices, distances, and wavelengths the question already gives.
3. Measure incidence and refraction angles from the normal, not from the surface.
4. Apply the matching formula and keep the lens sign convention that came with the data.
5. Solve for the unknown and state the unit.
6. If a focal length, index, distance, or wavelength is missing, stop and name that missing value.

## Formulas

- **Law of reflection**: `θi = θr` — The angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection, both measured from the normal.
- **Snell's law**: `n1 sin θ1 = n2 sin θ2` — Refraction relates each index to the sine of its angle from the normal.
- **Thin lens equation**: `1/f = 1/do + 1/di` — A thin lens relates focal length to object distance and image distance.
- **Magnification**: `m = hi/ho = -di/do` — Image height over object height equals the negative ratio of image distance to object distance.
- **Wave speed**: `v = fλ` — Wave speed in a medium equals frequency times wavelength in that medium.

## Checks

- Reflection angles are equal only when both are measured from the normal.
- Snell's law matches n sin θ on each side of the boundary.
- Use 1/f = 1/do + 1/di and m = hi/ho = -di/do on the same pair of distances.
- v = fλ uses the speed in that medium; frequency is unchanged at a boundary while wavelength can change.

## Limits

- Ray formulas do not give a diffraction spacing or fringe position that was not provided.
- The thin-lens equation is not a thick-lens or multi-element prescription.
- This card does not supply a focal length, index, or distance the user did not give.
