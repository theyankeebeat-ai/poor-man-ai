# Mechanics

This card solves force, motion, momentum, work, energy, and torque questions from the numbers already given.

## Method

1. Identify the unknown as force, motion, momentum, work, energy, or torque.
2. Write only the mass, velocity, time, force, angle, and displacement the question gives.
3. Pick the positive direction from the problem and keep every sign on that axis.
4. Use the constant-acceleration kinematics only when acceleration is constant.
5. Match net force to F = ma, impulse to J = Δp, and a lever to τ = r F sin θ.
6. If mass, g, a friction coefficient, or a component is missing, name it instead of inventing it.

## Formulas

- **Newton's second law**: `F = ma` — Net force on a body equals its mass times its acceleration.
- **Momentum**: `p = mv` — Linear momentum is mass times velocity.
- **Impulse**: `J = Δp` — Impulse equals the change in momentum.
- **Weight**: `W = mg` — Weight is mass times the local gravitational acceleration.
- **Constant-acceleration velocity**: `v = v0 + at` — Final velocity after time t when acceleration is constant.
- **Constant-acceleration position**: `x = x0 + v0 t + (1/2) a t^2` — Position after time t when acceleration is constant.
- **Work**: `W = Fd cos θ` — Work is force times displacement times the cosine of the angle between them.
- **Kinetic energy**: `(1/2)mv^2` — Kinetic energy is one-half mass times speed squared.
- **Torque**: `τ = r F sin θ` — Torque uses the angle between the position vector and the force.

## Checks

- F = ma uses the net force on that mass, not one force among several.
- v = v0 + at and x = x0 + v0 t + (1/2) a t^2 need constant acceleration.
- Work is W = Fd cos θ, with θ between force and displacement.
- Torque uses sin θ between r and F; the factor is 1 only when they are perpendicular.

## Limits

- These relations treat the object as a point except for the torque definition given.
- Friction is not a coefficient times a normal force unless both are given.
- This card does not supply a mass, time, speed, or force the user did not give.
