# Psychology

**Scope:** A study card on behavior and mental processes, including learning and bias, not a diagnosis or a treatment.

## Method

1. Define the question as a behavior or a mental process, not as a label for a person.
2. Separate what was observed from how it is interpreted.
3. For classical conditioning, name CS, US, CR, and UR only when each one is present.
4. Treat working memory as limited and keep the new load small.
5. Ask whether confirmation bias is keeping only the agreeing evidence.
6. Say what this card does not establish about any individual.

## Formulas

- **Psychology:** The scientific study of behavior and mental processes. Use it for a study question, not for judging a person's worth or health.
- **Cognition:** Mental processing such as attention, remembering, and judgment. Name the process being discussed instead of using cognition as a vague label.
- **Unconditioned stimulus and response:** The US naturally elicits the UR, before any new learning. Food that produces salivation is the classic US, and that salivation is the UR.
- **Conditioned stimulus and response:** After pairing, the CS elicits the CR. A bell paired with food can become the CS, and salivation to the bell is the CR.
- **Classical conditioning:** A neutral stimulus paired with a US comes to elicit a CR related to the UR. Do not call it classical conditioning unless the pairing and the learned response are both identified.
- **Working memory:** A limited system that briefly holds and uses a small amount of information. Capacity is small and improves with chunking; do not treat it as unlimited storage.
- **Confirmation bias:** Favoring evidence that agrees with a belief already held. Look for search, interpretation, and recall that ignore or discount contrary evidence.

## Checks

- CS, US, CR, and UR are not swapped.
- A behavior claim is separated from a claim about a mental process.
- The evidence is not only the evidence that confirms the preferred idea.
- No person's condition is inferred from the definition alone.

## Limits

- Do not diagnose.
- Do not prescribe treatment.
- Do not claim a test score.
